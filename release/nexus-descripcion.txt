NOMBRE: Moonlighter Coop - Online multiplayer (2-5 players)
CATEGORÍA: Gameplay
VERSIÓN: 0.11.0
RESUMEN (corto): Play Moonlighter online with 2 to 5 friends: shared dungeons, shop, loot and gold, ghosts and revives, roles, a race mode, and colours for every part of Will.

DESCRIPCIÓN (BBCode):
[size=5][b]Moonlighter Coop[/b][/size]
Play [b]Moonlighter[/b] online with up to [b]5 players[/b] (Steam invite / 7-letter code, or direct IP). Menus in Spanish, drawn in the game's own style.

[b]Playing together[/b]
[list]
[*]Isaac-style rooms: anyone crosses a door and everybody goes. Shop and village too.
[*]Shared loot, backpack and gold. Enemies chase the nearest player; bosses and projectiles are synced.
[*]Downed players become ghosts; the living revive them in a fountain, with a potion or on the next floor.
[*]Shared shop: shelves, customers, thieves, cash and day/night. Forge, witch and town purchases are announced to everybody.
[*]Tutorial together, reconnect after a drop, joining mid-run, difficulty that scales with players.
[/list]

[b]Host rules (F2 → Reglas)[/b]
[list]
[*]Roles: Guardian, Fighter, Seeker, Healer.
[*]Wait for everyone at the boss door, competitive shop, one heart (if anyone falls the run ends), hard revive.
[*][b]Race mode[/b]: on normal floors each player plays his own copy of the same floor; you wait for each other at the way down and see who was first. Loot and gold are pooled.
[/list]

[b]More[/b]
[list]
[*]Give or ask for equipment, short chat (with an on-screen keyboard for the gamepad), 12 co-op achievements, run history, run and day summaries.
[*]Partners' portraits and health, name tags, slow-connection mark.
[*]F3: colour of every part of your Will or a whole style; save your own styles and share them with a code.
[*]Automatic backup of your saves before playing online.
[*]Full gamepad support (L3 + R3 opens the co-op menu).
[*]Warns when players run different mods (stack / inventory mods must match: the backpack is shared).
[/list]

[b]Install[/b]
Close the game, unzip and run [i]Instalar.bat[/i] (it installs BepInEx 5 and the mod). F2 = co-op menu, F3 = looks.

[b]Requirements[/b]
Moonlighter (Steam, Windows) 1.15.8. Every player needs the same version of this mod (the game tells you if not).

Source code, changelog and updates: https://github.com/Eldeallado/moonlighter-coop
