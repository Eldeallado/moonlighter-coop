NOMBRE: Moonlighter Coop - Online multiplayer (2-5 players)
CATEGORÍA: Gameplay
RESUMEN (corto): Play Moonlighter online with 2 to 5 friends: shared dungeons, shop, loot and day/night, ghosts and revives, colours for every part of Will.

DESCRIPCIÓN (BBCode):
[size=5][b]Moonlighter Coop[/b][/size]
Play [b]Moonlighter[/b] online with up to [b]5 players[/b] (Steam invite / 7-letter code, or direct IP).

[b]Features[/b]
[list]
[*]Isaac-style rooms: anyone crosses a door and everybody goes. Shop and village too.
[*]Shared loot and backpack, shared gold. Enemies chase the nearest player; bosses and projectiles are synced.
[*]Downed players become ghosts; the living revive them in a fountain or on the next floor.
[*]Shared shop: shelves, customers, thieves, cash and day/night.
[*]Tutorial together: each player plays it, seeing the others.
[*]Reconnect after a drop, version check, update notice, difficulty that scales with players, gamepad support.
[*]F3: colour of every part of your Will (vest, shirt, trousers, backpack, shoes, gem, hair, skin) or a whole style.
[*]Warns when players run different mods (stack / inventory mods must match: the backpack is shared).
[/list]

[b]Install[/b]
Close the game, unzip and run [i]Instalar.bat[/i] (it installs BepInEx 5 and the mod). F2 = co-op menu, F3 = looks.

[b]Requirements[/b]
Moonlighter (Steam, Windows) 1.15.8. Every player needs the same version of this mod.

Source code and updates: https://github.com/Eldeallado/moonlighter-coop
Built on top of the Co-op Anything idea. Author: elde_al_lado.
